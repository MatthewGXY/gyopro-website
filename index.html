What we do

Specialists in Gyprock Ceilings & Walls
for Commercial and Residential Projects.

  • Cornices, Square Set, P50

    Decorative cornices, clean square-set edges and architectural P50 shadow-line details for a finished look.

  • Insulation

    Acoustic and thermal insulation installed to BCA standards for comfort year-round.

  • Framing

    Steel and timber wall framing laid out precisely — the backbone of every partition we build.

  • Plasterboard

    Plasterboard sheeting hung, jointed and stopped ready for paint — straight, flush, durable.

  • Float and Set Rendering

    Two-coat cement render — floated and set to a true, paint-ready surface on internal and external walls.
